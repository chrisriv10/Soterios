Online protection services

Privacy and history

History contains findings—not a browsing log—and never includes incognito activity.

Never stored: plaintext passwords, full URLs, SHA-1 values, HIBP prefixes, unkeyed password hashes, API keys, analytics, telemetry, or tracking identifiers.
